# Case validation on public daily data

11 cases with a documented period were considered, 10 stocks are in the price panel (1D data, 1551 stocks, 2015-10-01 to 2025-09-30; about 1317 stocks have scores on a typical recent date). Window 10 trading days. Random-stock null: 300 stocks per case. Chance level: 0.5 superiority, 5% of cases with p < 0.05, and for the top-B lists the share of random stocks that reach the list on some date of the same period (reported next to each detector).
